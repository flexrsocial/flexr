2. What FLEXR does

  1. FLEXR puts users in contact with each other on the basis of mutual expressions of interest (“swipe”). Where interest is mutual, a “match” is created, after which a chat is possible.
  2. Which profiles are shown depends on the gym you have chosen and the search radius you have set. FLEXR does not collect any device location for this; the calculation uses the public address of the gym.
  3. What happens after that is up to the users. We do not guarantee any matches, any particular number of matches, or any success in finding a partner.
  4. FLEXR is a leisure service and not a matchmaking service in the sense of brokerage for a result.

3. Requirements for use

  1. You act as a consumer and use FLEXR exclusively for private purposes.

  4. You need a device with internet access, a valid email address and a working camera for the check under point 5.

4. Minimum age

  1. FLEXR is exclusively for persons aged 18 and over.
  2. At registration you state your date of birth; your age is calculated server-side (18 to 99 years are permitted). If it is below that, registration cannot be completed.
  3. Only a random device ID and the time are stored (details in the privacy policy).

5. Age and identity check before activation

  1. In addition to stating your date of birth, every new account goes through a one-off check before activation: you confirm your email address, take a verification selfie live with the camera and upload an image of a valid official photo ID (identity card, passport or driving licence).
  2. The check is carried out manually by a human. Profile photos, verification selfie and ID photo are checked by visual comparison and the date of birth is cross-checked. No automated biometric facial recognition is used, no facial features are computed or stored, and the rest of the document content is not read out.
  3. This is a visual check of the document presented, not an official identification procedure (no eID, no KYC). What we confirm with it is the plausibility of the age and of the profile belonging to that person, not the accuracy of all the profile details.
  4. The account is activated only once the check has been passed; only then are swiping, matching and chatting possible. The time the check takes costs you nothing — use is free of charge anyway (point 7).
  5. If the check is not successful — for example because the document is not legible, the person does not match, the date of birth differs or the person is not yet 18 — we ask for a new image or refuse activation. You will be told the reason.
  6. The verification selfie and the ID capture are deleted once the check is complete, whether it is approved or refused. Only the result is retained (details in the privacy policy, point 5).
  7. Where there is a reasonable suspicion of false information, we may block the account. We may ask existing accounts to undergo the check subsequently.

    • 20 likes per 24 hours. A “pass” (rejection) does not count towards it. The limit runs on a rolling basis: a like becomes available again 24 hours after it was given.
