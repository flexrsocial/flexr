Good to know

  • Cancelling is not deleting. Your free FLEXR account remains. To delete your account, use “Delete account” in the app.
  • When the subscription ends. An ordinary cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. Premium stays available until then; nothing is charged afterwards.
  • Store subscriptions. If you bought Premium in the App Store or on Google Play, Apple or Google is your contractual partner — please cancel in the store’s subscription settings. We still accept your statement here.
  • Withdrawal is something else. Within 14 days of purchase you can instead withdraw from the contract and get your money back.
