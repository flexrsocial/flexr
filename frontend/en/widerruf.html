Right of withdrawal

Information on the statutory right of withdrawal for paid FLEXR subscriptions.

Last updated: 17 August 2026

This is a translation provided for convenience. The German version is the legally binding one; if the two differ, the German wording prevails.

Four different things — please don’t mix them up

  • The free FLEXR account. Costs nothing — permanently. No chargeable contract arises, and the account never turns into a subscription automatically. Nor is there anything that expires and leaves the app locked.
  • Taking out FLEXR Premium. Only here does a chargeable contract arise: through your express order via “Get Premium” and the payment process that follows.
  • Cancelling Premium. Ends a running Premium subscription at the end of the period you have paid for. Possible at any time in the account area under “Manage / cancel subscription”. Your account then continues as a free account. You do not get back what you have already paid.
  • Withdrawal. Unwinds the contract within 14 days of conclusion, without you needing a reason. You get back what you have already paid. That is something different from cancellation — and it is what this page is for.

1. When you can withdraw at all

The statutory right of withdrawal for the paid FLEXR Premium exists in principle for 14 days from conclusion of the contract. No payment obligation arises for the free FLEXR account; it can be ended at any time by deleting the account. Mandatory statutory consumer rights remain unaffected.

Effects of withdrawal

If you withdraw from this contract, we must reimburse all payments received from you without delay and at the latest within fourteen days of the day on which we received notice of your withdrawal. For this reimbursement we use the same means of payment that you used for the original transaction, unless expressly agreed otherwise with you; in no case will you be charged any fees for this reimbursement.

If you requested that the service should begin during the withdrawal period, you must pay us a reasonable amount corresponding to the proportion of the service already provided up to the time you notified us, compared with the total scope of the service agreed in the contract.

3. Start of the service and expiry of the right of withdrawal

At your express request, the paid FLEXR service can begin before the 14-day withdrawal period has expired.

If you withdraw during that period, a reasonable pro-rata amount may be payable, under the statutory conditions, for the part of the service already provided up to the withdrawal.

In the case of a service, the right of withdrawal under Section 18(1)(1) of the Austrian Distance and Off-Premises Business Act (FAGG) expires only once the contract has been fully performed and — in the case of a chargeable contract — only under the conditions provided for by law.

Merely activating or using a FLEXR subscription that is still running does not in itself cause the right of withdrawal to expire.

6. Frequently asked questions

  
I only have a free account — do I need to withdraw?

  

    No. Using FLEXR is permanently free and does not become chargeable
    automatically. There is no contract you would have to withdraw from. If you
    want to get rid of your account, delete it in the account area.
  


  
I have a subscription and simply want to stop paying.

  

    Then cancellation is the right route: account area →
    “Manage / cancel subscription”. Access stays active until the end of the
    period you have paid for. If, on the other hand, you want your money back
    and it is less than 14 days since you took it out, withdrawal on this page is
    the right route.
  


  
Do I get all of my money back?

  

    If you withdraw within 14 days, we refund the fee. If you requested that the
    service begin immediately, we may retain the proportion attributable to the
    time actually used up to your statement. At 10 € per month that is
    around 33 cents per day.
  


  
How quickly do I get a confirmation?

  

    Immediately — the confirmation email is triggered as soon as you confirm
    above. If it doesn’t arrive, check your spam folder and write to us at
    flexr.social@proton.me to be on
    the safe side. Your statement takes effect when you send it, regardless of
    whether the email is delivered.
